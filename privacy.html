Cometfall TD: Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Who we are

Cometfall TD is made by Paper Comet Studio, which is responsible for your data in the game. Contact: contact.papercomet@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

The game saves your progress (shards, skill trees, unlocked maps, best results) and your settings on your device. We never receive this data. We have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers for the game, and we run no analytics of our own. Uninstalling the game removes the saved data.

Ads

Cometfall TD shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may process data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data and do not add anything to it. How Google handles it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings under Google or Privacy > Ads.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about ads through Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices. That entry appears only where the consent message applies.

Age

Cometfall TD is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, we can usually only point you to Google for the ad data it processes. Write to us if you have a question or a request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact
