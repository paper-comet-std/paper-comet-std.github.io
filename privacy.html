2. Short overview

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. We run no analytics of our own. We never receive data from the game. Ads in the game come from Google, which handles that data on its own responsibility. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies of ours.

3. Data that stays on your device

The game saves your progress (shards, skill trees, unlocked maps, best results), your settings and your ad consent choice on your device. We never receive this data. Saving it is needed for the game to work, so no consent is needed for it (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, D04 E5W5, Ireland. Google decides on its own how it uses this data: it is an independent controller, not a service provider of ours.

To show ads, measure them and stop fraud, Google may process data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. With your consent, Google can also show personalised ads. Personalised ads are based on a profile of your interests (profiling). We do not receive any of this data and do not add anything to it. How Google uses it: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). You choose whether Google may store and read data on your device and use your data for ads. "Consent" and "Do not consent" are equally easy.

Legal basis: your consent under § 25(1) TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. In the UK and Switzerland similar rules apply.

You do not have to agree. The game works the same either way. Without consent, Google may show limited ads or no ads. Limited ads use no personal data for personalisation. Google still uses data such as your IP address to deliver the ad and may use device storage to detect fraud. Google says it does this based on its own legitimate interests. We make no claim of our own to legitimate interests for ads.

Change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices. Withdrawing is as easy as agreeing. It applies from then on; processing before the withdrawal stays lawful (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

6. US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California), you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information and of targeted ads. Use Settings > Privacy choices in the game. After you opt out, Google uses restricted data processing for you: it shows no ads based on your past behaviour. You may still see ads based on the game you are playing.

7. Android ad settings

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your Android settings under Google or Privacy > Ads.

8. This website (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (GitHub Pages). When you open a page, GitHub logs your IP address for security. Technical connection data such as the time and the page you request are also processed. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We set no cookies and run no analytics on this site. GitHub decides how long it keeps its logs: GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. When you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete the email when your request is done, unless the law requires us to keep it. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

10. Transfers outside the EU

Google LLC and GitHub, Inc. in the USA may receive data. Both are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The EU Commission has decided that this gives an adequate level of data protection (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR).

11. How long data is kept

  • Game data and your consent choice: on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. We keep nothing.
  • Ad data: Google's own periods, see the Google links in section 4.
  • Website logs: GitHub's own periods, see the link in section 8.
  • Emails: until your request is done, unless the law requires longer.

12. Automated decisions

We make no automated decisions about you. Google uses profiling for personalised ads only with your consent. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect on you is made (Art. 22 GDPR).

13. Age

Cometfall TD is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. In many EU countries (in Germany: under 16) a parent must agree to consent for online services. If you are under that age, ask a parent before you choose "Consent", or choose "Do not consent".

Age signals from Google Play. When the game starts, it asks Google Play for an age signal. Google answers only where a law requires it (for example in California, Texas or Brazil) or where you or a parent chose to share it, and gives only an age range (such as 13–15), never your date of birth. We use the age range only to limit ads: players under 18 get no personalised ads and restricted data processing, players under 13 get only ads suitable for children. The signal is not stored, not sent to us and not passed on. The game never asks you for your age. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing younger players age-appropriate ads and in meeting legal duties outside the EU (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

14. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). Because we hold no personal data from the game, we can usually only point you to Google for ad data. Write to us with any question or request.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process data based on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f): website logs, emails, age signals), you can object at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation. We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate grounds or need the data for legal claims. An email to us is enough.

15. Complaints

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example where you live. The authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Düsseldorf, www.ldi.nrw.de.

16. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
